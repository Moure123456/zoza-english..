## 🌟 What is Zoza?

**Zoza** is a friendly talking robot with a living face. She **speaks English out loud**, **listens to you**, **answers your questions**, **corrects your mistakes**, and **leads the conversation** with fun questions, so that students learn English by simply chatting.

Built especially for **Arabic-speaking learners** 🇪🇬🇸🇦🇦🇪🇯🇴🇮🇶 — every reply comes with a simple Arabic translation.

## 🎙️ Hands-Free Voice Conversation

1. Tap the **🎙️ microphone once**.
2. Zoza **listens** 👂 → **thinks** 💭 → **answers with her voice** 🗣️ → **listens again** 🔁
3. No more tapping. Just talk like you would with a real friend.

You can also type ⌨️, replay any sentence 🔊, slow her down 🐢, or hide the Arabic translation 🇪🇬.

---

## 🧠 A Smart Conversation Partner

- ❓ **Answers your questions** first, then asks her own, so she leads the dialogue.
- 👤 **Uses your name** and remembers what you tell her (age, city, hobbies).
- 💬 **Reacts like a person**: she is happy, sorry, curious, or surprised.
- 🔄 **Never repeats the same sentence** until all her variations are used.
- ✏️ **Instant corrections** in Arabic for common mistakes, for example:
  - ❌ *I have 15 years* → ✅ *I am 15 years old*
  - ❌ *He go to school* → ✅ *He goes to school*
  - ❌ *I am agree* → ✅ *I agree*
  - ❌ *a apple* → ✅ *an apple*

## ⚙️ Quick Start

1. 🍴 Upload all files to a GitHub repository.
2. 🌐 **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**.
3. 🔗 Open your link in **Chrome** or **Edge** (HTTPS required for the microphone).
4. 🎙️ Allow the microphone, tap **Start**, and say *“Hello, Zoza!”*

> ⚠️ Safari on iPhone does not support speech recognition. Use Chrome or Edge on Android or desktop for the full voice experience.
